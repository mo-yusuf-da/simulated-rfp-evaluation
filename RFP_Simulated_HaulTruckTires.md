# Request for Proposal (RFP): Off-Road Haul Truck Tires

> **SIMULATED DOCUMENT.** This RFP is a fictional portfolio exercise. The buyer, the suppliers, and all figures are invented. It is not connected to any real company or procurement.

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| RFP reference | SIM-RFP-001 |
| Buyer | Fictional aggregates producer (Calgary, AB region) |
| Category | Off-road haul truck tires, radial 29.5R25 class |
| Contract term | 5 years, with an option to renew for 2 years |
| Estimated demand | 40 trucks x 6 tires, 3,000 operating hours per truck per year |

## 1. Purpose and scope
The buyer seeks a single primary supplier for haul truck tires, including delivery to the buyer's yard, onsite fitment support, and warranty administration. Bids are evaluated on total cost of ownership (TCO), not on unit price alone.

## 2. Timeline
| Milestone | Target |
| --- | --- |
| RFP issued | Day 0 |
| Supplier questions due | Day 7 |
| Bids due | Day 21 |
| Evaluation and reference checks | Day 22 to 35 |
| Recommendation to Director, Procurement | Day 38 |
| Contract execution | Day 60 |

## 3. Requirements
1. Tires must meet the stated size and rock-service rating, with a written life guarantee in operating hours.
2. Bidders must complete the pricing template (`Pricing_Template` sheet), including freight, annual escalation, payment terms, warranty credit, and lead time.
3. Bidders must provide a certificate of commercial liability insurance, a safety program summary, and three customer references with fleet-level tire life data.
4. Warranty credits for premature failure must be paid within 30 days of an approved claim.
5. Any exception to the draft agreement must be listed in the bid.

## 4. Evaluation criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Total cost of ownership (5-year NPV) | 40% |
| Technical performance and quality | 20% |
| Delivery and service | 15% |
| Supplier risk and compliance | 10% |
| Commercial terms | 10% |
| Sustainability and safety practices | 5% |

Each non-cost criterion is scored 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). The TCO score is 5 x (lowest NPV TCO / bidder NPV TCO). The weighted score determines ranking. Sensitivity testing confirms the result holds under alternative weightings.

## 5. TCO components
Purchase cost (with escalation), freight, onsite support fees, downtime cost from premature failures, warranty credits, and the working-capital benefit of payment terms, discounted to present value at the buyer's cost of capital.

## 6. Submission
Bids are submitted electronically in the pricing template format. Late or incomplete bids may be rejected. Bids must remain valid for 90 days.
